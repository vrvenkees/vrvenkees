How the terminal works

NovaX combines public market data with a paper-trading ledger. Prices and trades shown in the terminal are not financial advice and paper orders never move real assets.

Market order

A paper market order executes immediately against the latest displayed price.

Limit order

A paper limit order stays open until the live market reaches its price or you cancel it.

NovaX is a market-data and paper-trading sandbox. It does not custody funds or execute real cryptocurrency trades.
